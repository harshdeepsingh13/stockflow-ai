# Description and Keyword Best Practices

Excellent metadata (descriptions and keywords) is a crucial element in ensuring customers can discover your content when searching on **Shutterstock.com.** Here are some best practices to help you make the most of your metadata.

Your description (title) and keywords serve different purposes and callfor different approaches, but some guidelines apply equally to both. For example, always be respectful and accurate when representingpeople's identities, and never include personally identifiable information.

## Description/Title

Great content descriptions (titles) are unique and detailed. They read like a sentence or phrase and not like a list of words. Think of your descriptions as news headlines: try to answer the main questions of Who,What, When, Where, and Why, but also capture the mood andemotion behind your content.

# Content Publishing Standards: Contextual

## Metadata

Descriptions and keywords, along with other relevant tags are essential to making your content discoverable.

Updated over 2 months ago

### Table of contents

Description/title, keyword, category, and location information are collectively called "metadata." Metadata drives Shutterstock's search and discovery tools and helps customers find the content they are looking for in our vast collection of photos, illustrations, vectors, and videos.

### Metadata Requirements

#### Description/Title

·A description/title is a factual sentence that describes the content's subject matter. When submitting documentary editorial content, descriptions also must include the following:

。 The day, month, and year the content was taken. For content shot before the year 2000,only the year/decade is required.

。The geographical location where the image was taken.

·Descriptions/titles must be relevant and applicable to the content you are submitting.

·The maximum length of a description/title is 2048 characters.

·Descriptions/titles must be in English, however, exceptions are made for scientific Latin names of plants and animals, names of places, and foreign terms or phrases commonly used in the English language.

·Description should be 1000 characters at minimum. Minimum character count for description is 1000.

·Description should be 1900 characters at max. Maximum character count for description is 1900.

#### Keywords

Keywords are individual words or compound words that describe the content's subject matter.

·Keywords must be relevant and applicable to the content you are submitting.

·A minimum of 7 keywords are required and the maximum is 50.

·Keywords must be in English, however, exceptions are made for scientific Latin names of plants and animals, names of places, and foreign terms or phrases commonly used in the English language.

### Categories

Categories organize content into groups that are based on a similar theme or subject. Shutterstock's category options and definitions can be found at the bottom of this page.

·Categories must be relevant and applicable to the content you are submitting.

·1 category is required; a second category is optional.

#### Location

Location is the geographic location that is shown in the content.

·Location information is optional, but if provided, be as specific as you can. Select neighborhoods, towns, or cities if possible.

·Location information must be relevant and applicable to the content you are submitting.

Content wvill be rejected for Metadata - Commercial Requirements or Metadata - Editorial Requirements if the associated metadata does not relate to the depicted subject matter. The Metadata - Editorial Requirements rejection reason is also used if the year or geographical location is inaccurate or missing from a documentary editorial description.

### Additional Description/Title & Keyword Requirements

·Metadata must comply with Shutterstock's Intellectual Property. title and keyword guidelines. For example, descriptions and keywords associated with commercial content cannot contain trademarks such as brand names or product names.

·Content that relates to race, ethnicity, disabilities, age, religion, gender, or sexual orientation should accurately and respectfully represent people's identities. See our Guidelines for Submitting Diverse and Inclusive Content article for additional information.

·Terminology that is offensive, hateful, racist, derogatory, discriminatory, or disrespectful is strictly prohibited. See our Objectionable or Explicit article for more information.

·Never include sensitive, personally identifiable information such as social security numbers or financial information in titles or keywords.

·Titles and keywords should be free of spelling errors, grammatical errors, emojis, and spam.

·Do not include case numbers in titles or keywords.
